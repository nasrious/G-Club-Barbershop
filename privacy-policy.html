G Club Barbershop ("we," "us") operates gclubpattaya.com. This page explains what information we collect when you visit or book with us, and how we use it.

Information we collect

When you book an appointment through our site, our booking partner Setmore collects your name, phone number, email address, and appointment details. We use this only to manage your booking and contact you about it.

We use Vercel Analytics to understand general site traffic (pages visited, approximate location by country/city, device type). This data is anonymous and not tied to your identity.

How we use your information

  • To confirm and manage your appointment
  • To contact you about your booking if needed
  • To understand how visitors use our site, so we can improve it

We do not sell your information to anyone.

Third parties
